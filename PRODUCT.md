# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Students, participants in hackathons and competitions, indie creators, and people who want to build bespoke websites for themselves with a direct, hands-on visual canvas—without writing code and without accepting generic AI-generated templates.

## Product Purpose
Pickle Studio is a tactile, code-free visual website builder and publishing platform engineered by Pickle Corp™. It empowers creators to design responsive, production-grade websites visually on an interactive canvas and publish them instantly with custom slugs and hosting.

## Positioning
"No AI. No Code." Built for people who want the craft and satisfaction of designing their own websites with complete spatial control, free from black-box AI slop or subscription paywalls.

## Operating Context
- High-density web workbench featuring a sub-pixel spatial canvas with multi-breakpoint responsive preview (Desktop, Laptop, Tablet, Mobile).
- Used in desktop browsers for building personal portfolios, competition entries, community projects, and showcase landing pages.
- Dual export pipeline: clean, standalone semantic HTML5/CSS bundle export and 1-click publishing via Pickle Cloud Service.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Spatial canvas editing: magnetic snapping, distance measurement, guides, marquee multi-select, and grouping.
- Rich component library: semantic blocks, starter kits, form leads, e-commerce drawer, and physics motion animations.
- Pickle Cloud Service: centralized managed publishing, slug routing, QR preview codes, and real-time visitor analytics.
- Clean code export: standalone semantic HTML/CSS with embedded keyframes and zero third-party framework dependencies.
- **Durable Constraints**: Must preserve existing editor functionality, core tools, company name (Pickle Corp™ / Pickle Studio), and the brand logo. Full redesign permitted for surrounding UI, typography, color architecture, and marketing surfaces.

## Brand Commitments
- **Name**: Pickle Corp™ / Pickle Studio
- **Logo & Marks**: Preserve the signature brand logo and identity mark.
- **Tone**: Bold, craft-obsessed, direct, energetic, and unapologetically anti-slop. High-velocity workbench ergonomics for builders.

## Product Principles
1. **Direct Manipulation First**: Every element responds to tactile, spatial interaction rather than algorithmic guesswork.
2. **Zero-Slop Craft**: Eliminates generic AI tropes (purple gradients, rubbery bounce animations, fake window chrome) in favor of distinctive, intentional aesthetics.
3. **Beginner Accessibility with Power-Tool Precision**: Friendly enough for a student building their first project; capable enough for sub-pixel layout and custom breakpoints.
4. **Sovereign Code**: Exported sites are 100% self-contained, clean HTML5/CSS with no vendor lock-in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Full WCAG AA contrast compliance across dark and light surfaces.
- Visible, high-contrast keyboard navigation rings for all canvas controls and dialogs.
- Semantic HTML tags emitted across all exported layouts.
